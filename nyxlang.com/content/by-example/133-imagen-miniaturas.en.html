A shop uploads a product photo from the phone — 3 to 5 MB, 12 megapixels, sometimes rotated by its EXIF orientation — and the system has to serve it small: about 600 px for the product page, about 200 px for the list, as JPEG, without the photo's location. std/image is pure Nyx, native and wasm32-wasi, so neither libjpeg nor ImageMagick is needed. The sample photo is built inside the recipe with image_encode_jpeg; in real life it is the bytes of the multipart that arrived.

original: jpeg 1600x1200, 140360 bytes ficha: jpeg 600x450, 13167 bytes tamaño 0: jpeg 600x450, 13167 bytes tamaño 1: jpeg 200x150, 3175 bytes tamaño 2: jpeg 80x60, 1366 bytes calidad 95: 8025 bytes calidad 80: 3168 bytes calidad 60: 2504 bytes calidad 40: 2174 bytes logo: jpeg 64x64 (sobre blanco) rechazada (invalid): image_thumbnail: no parece una imagen JPEG ni PNG (firma desconocida) rechazada (invalid): image_thumbnail: la calidad va de 1 a 100 (llegó 101)

image_thumbnail decodes, shrinks and encodes in ONE step, decoding at the largest possible reduction (1/2, 1/4 or 1/8) so a 12 MP photo is not decoded in full. image_thumbnails makes several sizes from a SINGLE decode — the product page, the list and an 80 px one. Quality runs 1 to 100 (image_default_quality() is 80) and its effect on the bytes is visible in the four calidad lines.

A PNG with transparency, the logo, comes out composited over white, which is what a JPEG can store; for another background call image_flatten before encoding. Errors are a Result with a kind: "invalid" (a parameter or a limit), "parse" (a damaged file) and "unsupported" (HEIC, WebP, progressive JPEG...). Both rejections at the end of the output are invalid.
